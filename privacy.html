PRIVACY POLICY

Olympus Siege  ·  Effective 11 August 2026

Olympus Siege ("the game", "we") is a mobile game published by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and what your choices are. The short version: the game collects no email address, no real name, no contacts, no location, and runs no analytics or tracking. The only data that ever leaves your device is what powers the global leaderboard.

1. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

All of your game progress — currencies, upgrades, relics, achievements, settings, and the current defense — is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to us or anyone else, except for the specific leaderboard data described below.

2. DATA STORED ON OUR SERVERS

To operate the global leaderboard and let you move your profile between devices, our servers store a minimal profile:

DataPurpose
Defender name — a display name you choose yourself Shown on the global leaderboard next to your score
Gameplay statistics — your best wave reached Your leaderboard ranking
Hashed identifiers — a random, game-generated profile ID stored in hashed form Ties your score to your profile; enables device transfer

That is the complete list. The defender name is free-form text of your choosing and does not need to be (and shouldn't be) your real name. No email address, account registration, password, device fingerprint, advertising ID, or location data is collected.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) in the europe-west1 region (Belgium, European Union). Google acts as our hosting processor; the data is not shared with, sold to, or used by anyone else.

3. WHAT IS PUBLIC

The leaderboard is public by design: your chosen defender name and best wave are visible to other players in the game's leaderboard screen. Nothing else about you is visible to anyone.

4. ANALYTICS, TRACKING & ADVERTISING

The game contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party advertising in its current version. If a future version adds optional rewarded ads or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated first and the new version's effective date changed accordingly.

5. PURCHASES

If you buy something through an app store (Google Play or the App Store), the payment is processed entirely by that store under its own privacy policy — we never see your payment details. If a web shop opens on this site, its payments will be processed by a dedicated merchant of record under its own policy, and we will only receive what is needed to grant the purchase to your game profile.

6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Your server-side profile is kept for as long as it remains on the leaderboard system. You can request deletion of your server-side data (defender name, score, and identifiers) at any time by emailing olympussiege.dev@gmail.com — include your defender name and, if possible, your transfer code so we can locate the profile. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Local data on your device can be removed at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since the only personal data we hold is the short list in section 2, any such request can be handled quickly — email us at the address above.

8. CHILDREN

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only free-form data the game accepts is the defender name; if you believe a child has submitted personal information as a defender name, contact us and we will remove it.

9. SECURITY

Server-side data is held in Google Cloud infrastructure, accessible only through the game's own backend functions — there is no direct public access to the database. Profile identifiers are stored hashed, so a database leak would not expose usable profile secrets.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the game's data practices change (for example, if optional rewarded ads are added), we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.

11. CONTACT

Olympus Siege — olympussiege.dev@gmail.com
